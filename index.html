How to Apply

Your journey to a rewarding technical career starts here

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your place at Kiharu TVC:

1

Choose Your Programme

Chose the programme that aligns with your career goals. Diploma (level 6), Certificate (level 5), Artisan (level 4) and KCPE certificate (Level 3)

2

Collect Application Form

Pick up a physical application form at the Registrar's Office or download one from our website.

Collect Application Form
3

Submit Documents

Submit KCSE / KCPE certificate / result slip, National ID / Birth Certificate, 2 passport photos and application fee receipt.

4

Await Admission Letter

Successful applicants will receive an official admission letter within 5 working days.

5

Report & Register

On the reporting date, clear fees, collect your student ID and officially register for your programme.

Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade C-and above for diploma programmes (Level 6)
  • KCSE Mean Grade D and above for Certificate courses (Level 5)
  • KCSE Mean Grade D- and below for Artisan Certificate courses (Level 4)
  • KCPE and above for Level 3 courses

Intake Dates

  • January Intake
  • May Intake
  • September
  • Rolling admissions for short courses

Board Chairman
Board of Governors — Kiharu TVC
Chairman's Remarks

On behalf of the Board of Governors, I extend my warmest welcome to all students, parents, staff and partners of Kiharu Technical & Vocational College. Our institution was founded on the firm belief that technical and vocational education is the cornerstone of national development and individual empowerment.

We remain committed to providing a learning environment that is not only academically rigorous but also practically oriented — ensuring that every graduate leaves our institution equipped with the skills, character and confidence to thrive in the modern world.

I encourage all students to embrace every opportunity presented to them, engage fully with their programmes, and conduct themselves with integrity. To our dedicated staff, your commitment to excellence continues to be the foundation of our success. Together, we will continue to build an institution that our community is proud of.

Board Chairman
Board of Governors, Kiharu TVC
