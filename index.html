How to Apply

Your journey to a rewarding technical career starts here

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your place at Kiharu TVC:

1

Choose Your Programme

Browse our undergraduate and certificate programmes and select the one that aligns with your career goals.

2

Collect Application Form

Pick up a physical application form at the Registrar's Office or download one from our website.

3

Submit Documents

Submit KCSE certificate / result slip, National ID / Birth Certificate, 2 passport photos and application fee receipt.

4

Await Admission Letter

Successful applicants will receive an official admission letter within 5 working days.

5

Report & Register

On the reporting date, clear fees, collect your student ID and officially register for your programme.

📋

Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade D+ and above for certificate courses
  • KCSE Mean Grade C- and above for diploma programmes
  • Relevant craft certificate for artisan upgrading
  • Mature age entry (25+) considered on merit
💰

Application Fees

A non-refundable application fee of KES 500 is payable at the accounts office or via M-Pesa Paybill.

  • M-Pesa Paybill: 522 533
  • Account No: Student ID / Name
  • Office hours: Mon–Fri, 8AM–5PM
📅

Intake Dates

  • January Intake — Applications: Oct–Dec
  • May Intake — Applications: Feb–Apr
  • September Intake — Applications: Jun–Aug
  • Rolling admissions for short courses

Research & Innovation

Applied research connecting the classroom to the community

🔬

Our Research Philosophy

At Kiharu TVC, we believe that technical education must be rooted in real-world application. Our research programme connects students, faculty and industry to solve practical problems in engineering, ICT, agriculture and community development.

⚡

Renewable Energy Research

Student-led projects in solar PV installation, biogas systems and energy-efficient building design in collaboration with local communities.

🤖

Digital Innovation Lab

An ICT innovation hub where students develop apps, automation systems and cybersecurity solutions for local businesses and institutions.

🌱

Community Applied Research

Partnering with NGOs and county government on water, sanitation, construction and social welfare projects in Murang'a County.

📰

Student Publications

Annual technical journal showcasing final-year student projects, innovations and applied research across all schools.

Mozzira Foundation

Empowering lives through education, skills and community development

❤️ The Mozzira Foundation

The Mozzira Foundation is the social arm of Kiharu TVC, dedicated to expanding access to quality technical and vocational training for disadvantaged youth and communities across Murang'a County and beyond.

🎓

Scholarship Programme

Annual scholarships for academically deserving but financially disadvantaged students. Applications open every October.

👩‍💼

Women in Tech Initiative

Sponsoring girls and women from marginalised backgrounds to enroll in STEM programmes at Kiharu TVC.

🏘️

Community Skills Drive

Mobile skills training units that visit rural communities to offer free short courses in plumbing, solar, cookery and digital literacy.

🤝

Corporate Partnerships

Partner with us through CSR funding, equipment donations, internship slots or mentorship of our students and graduates.
