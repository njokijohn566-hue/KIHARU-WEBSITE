How to Apply

Your journey to a rewarding technical career starts here

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your place at Kiharu TVC:

1

Choose Your Programme

Browse our undergraduate and certificate programmes and select the one that aligns with your career goals.

2

Collect Application Form

Pick up a physical application form at the Registrar's Office or download one from our website.

3

Submit Documents

Submit KCSE certificate / result slip, National ID / Birth Certificate, 2 passport photos and application fee receipt.

4

Await Admission Letter

Successful applicants will receive an official admission letter within 5 working days.

5

Report & Register

On the reporting date, clear fees, collect your student ID and officially register for your programme.

Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade D+ and above for certificate courses
  • KCSE Mean Grade C- and above for diploma programmes
  • Relevant craft certificate for artisan upgrading
  • Mature age entry (25+) considered on merit

Application Fees

A non-refundable application fee of KES 500 is payable at the accounts office or via M-Pesa Paybill.

  • M-Pesa Paybill: 522 533
  • Account No: Student ID / Name
  • Office hours: Mon–Fri, 8AM–5PM

Intake Dates

  • January Intake — Applications: Oct–Dec
  • May Intake — Applications: Feb–Apr
  • September Intake — Applications: Jun–Aug
  • Rolling admissions for short courses

Job Adverts

Current vacancies at Kiharu Technical & Vocational College

Kiharu TVC is an equal-opportunity employer. We encourage applications from all qualified candidates. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted. Applications should be submitted to the Principal's Office or via email as indicated on each advert.

Lecturer — Computing & Informatics

Open
School of ICT & Business Full-Time Main Campus, Kiambugi

We are seeking a qualified and experienced Lecturer in Computing & Informatics to deliver TVET CDACC and KNEC programmes. The successful candidate will teach programming, networking, database management and cyber security modules, and contribute to departmental research and community engagement activities.

  • Diploma or Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field
  • TVET instructor certificate or equivalent teaching qualification
  • Minimum 2 years teaching or industry experience
  • Registered with TVETA as a trainer (added advantage)
Application Deadline: 30 July 2026 Apply via Email

Instructor — Mechanical & Automotive Engineering

Open
School of Engineering Full-Time Main Campus, Kiambugi

We are looking for a hands-on Mechanical & Automotive Engineering Instructor to train students in workshop practice, engine overhaul, fabrication and automotive systems. The candidate must be comfortable working with workshop equipment and guiding students through practical assessments.

  • Diploma in Mechanical or Automotive Engineering (Degree preferred)
  • Minimum Grade Test II / Craft Certificate in the relevant trade
  • At least 3 years workshop or industry experience
  • TVET instructor registration is an advantage
Application Deadline: 30 July 2026 Apply via Email

Accounts Assistant

Open
Finance & Accounts Full-Time Main Campus, Kiambugi

The Accounts Assistant will support the Finance Office in day-to-day financial transactions, student fees receipting, bank reconciliations and preparation of financial reports. The role reports directly to the Bursar.

  • CPA Part II or Diploma in Accounting / Finance
  • Proficiency in accounting software (QuickBooks or similar)
  • Strong attention to detail and integrity
  • At least 1 year relevant experience in a similar institution
